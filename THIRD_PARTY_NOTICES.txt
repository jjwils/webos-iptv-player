mpegts.js
---------
https://github.com/xqq/mpegts.js
Copyright (C) 2016 Bilibili and contributors; Apache-2.0.
Modified: the distributed bundle is patched at build time
(scripts/mpegts-patch.mjs) to recognize DVB AC-3 and E-AC-3 audio, merge
dependent E-AC-3 substreams into their frame, and correct the E-AC-3
dec3 data rate and sample duration.
Full upstream license:
  licenses/mpegts.js.txt
The bundle includes es6-promise, Copyright (c) 2014 Yehuda Katz, Tom Dale,
Stefan Penner and contributors; MIT.

Generated runtime helpers
-------------------------

service contains helpers inserted by TypeScript (such as __assign and
__importStar). These helpers are distributed code, not development tools.
Their license texts are included:
esbuild: https://github.com/evanw/esbuild (MIT)
  licenses/esbuild.txt
TypeScript: https://github.com/microsoft/TypeScript (Apache-2.0)
  licenses/typescript.txt
